Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026　/　App name: DokoOita

This Privacy Policy explains how the developer ("we", "us", or "our") handles user information in the Android application "DokoOita" (the "App").

1. Information the App Handles

The App handles the following information.

[Information entered by the user]

  • Photos (taken with the camera or selected from the gallery)
  • Videos (recorded with the App's camera, including sound)
  • Text entered by voice input or manually
  • Numerical data such as dates, prices and quantities
  • An API key for AI receipt reading (entered by the user)

[Information handled automatically]

  • Information related to ad delivery (see "4. Advertising")

We do not collect names, addresses, phone numbers, location, or similar information through the App. No account is required.

2. Where Your Data Is Stored

Everything you enter in the App (photos, videos, text, budget entries, receipts, shopping memos, end-of-life records, and so on) is stored only on your device. We do not send this data to our servers, and we do not receive or keep it.

Depending on your device's backup settings, Android's own mechanisms (such as Google backup) may save app data to your Google account. This depends on your device settings, and we do not receive it.

3. Permissions the App Uses

  • Camera: to take photos and record videos
  • Microphone: to record sound when recording videos
  • Internet: for ad delivery and the optional AI receipt reading
  • Advertising ID: for ad delivery (see "4. Advertising")
  • Write to storage: on Android 9 and earlier only, to save files to folders such as Download and Pictures

Voice input is a function of your phone's keyboard (the OS speech recognition), not of the App. The App itself does not record or send your voice for voice input. Voice data is handled according to the policy of the provider of your OS and keyboard (Google, the device manufacturer, etc.).

4. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to show ads. For this purpose, Google may collect information such as:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • Device type and OS version
  • Approximate location (based on IP address)
  • App usage and diagnostic information

Ads are shown, starting 7 days after installation, when you switch screens, at most once every 6 hours.

In the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and some other regions, a consent screen (Google's messaging tool) is shown before ads. You can change your choice later.

You can turn off ad personalization and reset your advertising ID in your device's "Settings" → "Google" → "Ads". For how Google handles information, see Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. AI Receipt Reading (Optional)

"Bulk register from receipts" offers an optional AI reading feature. To use it, you choose one of the following services and enter your own API key (a key you obtain from the service provider) in the App:

  • Anthropic (Claude)
  • Google (Gemini API)

When you choose "AI reading" and take a photo of a receipt, the image is sent directly from your device to the server of the service you chose. The service reads the store name, date, item names and prices and returns them to the App.

  • AI reading runs only when you choose it.
  • If you choose manual entry, no image is sent outside the App.
  • The image does not pass through our servers. We do not receive or keep the image.
  • Your API key is stored only on your device and is used only to authenticate with the service you chose. It is not included in export files such as ZIP.
  • Your account and API key are subject to the terms of the service you chose (which may set age or other requirements). How the image is handled is determined by that service's terms and privacy policy.

[Important note when using Google (Gemini API)]

According to Google's terms, when the Gemini API is used under the free tier (without a billing account), the content sent (such as receipt images) may be used by Google to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. This does not apply to paid use with a billing account. According to Google's terms, for users in the European Economic Area, Switzerland and the United Kingdom, the terms for paid use apply to all use, including the free quota. Because receipts contain shopping information, please take this into account when choosing a service, or use manual entry instead.

Terms and privacy policies of the services:

  • Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • Google Gemini API Additional Terms
  • Google Privacy Policy

6. Exporting and Sharing Data

The App has functions that let you take data out of the App:

  • PDF and CSV export (saved to folders such as Download/Kakeibo)
  • ZIP export, including photos (saved to Download/DokoOita)
  • Saving photos and videos (saved to Pictures/DokoOita and Movies/DokoOita)
  • Sharing via apps such as LINE and email

These happen only when you start them. Because they are saved in shared folders on your phone, other apps or people with access to your phone may be able to see them. What happens to data after sharing is the responsibility of you and the receiving service.

7. Disclosure to Third Parties

Except as required by law, we do not disclose user information to third parties. However, as described above, necessary information is sent to Google for ad delivery, and to the AI service you chose (Anthropic or Google) only when you use AI receipt reading.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to any specific age group. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

9. Security

Because the App stores data on your device, the security of the device itself (screen lock, keeping the OS up to date, etc.) is the most important. Please manage your device appropriately.

10. Deleting Your Data

You can delete all the data you registered from the App's settings. Uninstalling the App also removes its data from your phone. Images sent for AI reading are handled under the terms of the service you chose, so please contact that service about deleting them.

Files you exported or saved (see "6. Exporting and Sharing Data") remain in the shared folders on your phone. Please delete them yourself when you no longer need them.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as needed. Any changes will be published on this page, and the "Last updated" date will be revised.
